Pacific Costa Rica

🐢 Reserva Playa Tortuga

Sea turtle nesting monitoring across every crawl, every clutch, every season.

The record

Every nest, every season

Field teams patrol the coastline from May through November, logging every nesting event. The multi-year data reveals how activity has shifted over time.

Who nests here

Three species, one coastline

Olive Ridley, Green, and Hawksbill turtles each follow their own seasonal rhythm. The August - October window sees all three species nesting simultaneously at peak.

The threat

Not every nest survives

Poaching is tracked alongside nesting. Months where more than 15% of total nest activity was lost to poachers are flagged, showing where and when the reserve's protection work matters most.

Where they nest

Different beaches, different stories

Tortuga and Hermosa carry the bulk of nesting activity, each beach with its own trend.

Closing the loop

From egg to ocean

Eggs at highest risk are moved to the on-site hatchery. Season-by-season fertility, emergence, and hatchling release counts are tracked to help observe the effectiveness of conservation efforts.
